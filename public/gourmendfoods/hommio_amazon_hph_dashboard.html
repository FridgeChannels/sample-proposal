HOMMIO · AMAZON HOUSEHOLD ECONOMICS

The Economics of a Hommio Repeat-Purchase Household

Hommio Repeat-Purchase Household (HPH): a household that reorders from a brand through Hommio, placing at least two attributed orders within a specified year. This model routes those orders to Amazon.

BRB is a referral-fee credit, not sales revenue or a cash payment. The model assumes a 10% credit on all modeled attributed sales. Actual eligibility, category rate, qualifying sales and credit timing must be verified. A zero or negative net program cost does not mean zero total brand costs or guaranteed profit.

One household throughout. Year 2 assumes the same HPH places two attributed orders, with unchanged AOV, no attrition, and no new or replacement hardware. The two-year view sums both years, not a third year.

Pricing and allocation: 10,000 magnets × $0.60 = $6,000. At an assumed 40% weekly engagement and 30% conversion to HPH, 1,200 HPHs share the investment: $5 per HPH, counted once. Amazon commission is 5% of attributed net sales. Optional design fees and other deployment expenses are excluded.

Illustrative assumptions, not observed results. Exposure assumes five potential views per day; taps assume one per week. Neither measures actual attention or demonstrates engagement. Four orders from 104 taps would imply 3.85% tap-to-order conversion, which requires real user data to validate. Attributed sales are not a no-Hommio baseline or proof of incremental sales.

Not profit ROI. The revenue-to-cost multiple uses hardware plus Hommio commission before BRB. Do not divide by net program cost after BRB when it is zero or negative. Product costs, fulfillment, Amazon marketplace fees before the BRB offset, payment fees, tax and other brand expenses are excluded from the modeled remaining amount.
