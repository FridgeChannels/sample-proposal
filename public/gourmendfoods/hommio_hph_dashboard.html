Revenue per dollar of Hommio cost is not profit ROI. Product costs, fulfillment, payment fees, tax, and other brand expenses are not included; attributed revenue is not necessarily incremental.

One household throughout. Year 2 assumes the same HPH continues to place at least two orders in that year, with unchanged AOV, exposure and weekly interaction, no attrition, and no new or replacement hardware. The two-year view sums both years; it is not a third year.

Establishment cost basis: 10,000 magnets × $0.60 = $6,000. At an assumed 40% weekly engagement and 30% conversion to HPH, 1,200 HPHs share the investment: $6,000 ÷ 1,200 = $5 per HPH, allocated once. Other deployment expenses are not included.

Illustrative model, not observed results. Exposure and taps are modeled assumptions, not measured attention or demonstrated engagement. Exposure assumes five potential views per day with continued placement; taps assume one per week. Four attributed orders from 104 taps would imply a 3.85% tap-to-order conversion rate, not a validated result. Real user data is required to establish actual conversion. Attributed orders are not a no-HOMMIO baseline or proof of incremental sales.
