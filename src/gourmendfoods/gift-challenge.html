Channel Strategy

One Growth Engine.
Two Channel Plays.

Every play uses Hommios to increase the Hommio-Connected Household Rate—the leading indicator for building Hommio Retention Households (HRHs). HRHs are the repeat-purchase outcome, defined by at least two attributed orders in a specified year. The growth logic is shared; the emphasis shifts by channel.

01

DTC — Turn first orders into lasting relationships.

Move first orders to repeat orders, repeat customers to subscribers, and subscriber relationships into cross-sell.

02

Amazon — Grow LTV.

Turn Amazon-acquired buyers into Hommio-Connected Households, support repeat purchases and subscriptions, and build a more portable brand relationship through a programmable Reorder Magnet.

Across both channels, the growth engine is the same: increase Hommio-Connected Households Rate for the selected channel. Each channel play emphasizes a different path to repeat purchase and greater value over time.

Encourage repeat purchases, grow subscriptions, support subscriber retention and win-back, and create cross-sell and upsell opportunities.

Select segments and shipments around the brand’s goals. Former subscribers can use a Reorder Magnet they already have at home; they do not need a current subscription shipment.

  1. Choose the goal and customer segment. Identify the brand’s retention goal, select the relevant customer group, and plan an appropriate post-tap experience.
  2. Add the branded magnet alongside products in the DTC shipping box. If a third-party logistics provider is used, confirm it can support the selected insert workflow.
  3. Connect in the home. Customers keep the magnet at home and tap it with their phone when they want to return to the brand.
  4. Configure the experience. Connect the DTC store and relevant marketing data for the intended experience. Hommio’s DTC capabilities include Shopify coupon connections and Klaviyo customer segments; confirm the configuration for the brand’s setup. Tailor content using customer permission, available data, and configured segmentation.
  5. Measure and improve. Track engagement, coupon claims and redemptions, and attributed sales as applicable. Use the brand’s lifecycle data and, where feasible, a holdout comparison to assess incremental retention impact.

Solution for Amazon

Help Amazon brands stay within reach at home, make repeat purchases easier, support customer lifetime value, and learn from what customers choose to share—while keeping purchases on Amazon.

Build Lasting Customer Relationships

Stay within reach with useful product guidance and a relevant, updatable experience.
  1. Build the deployment plan. Choose the products, timing, magnet design, and experience that fit the brand’s retention goals and Amazon operation.
  2. Include the Reorder Magnet in the product package. Place the branded magnet inside the product packaging during production or packaging.
  3. Connect in the home. Customers receive the product and branded magnet, keep it somewhere convenient, and tap when they want product information or a way back to the brand.
  4. Review and configure the experience. Choose an appropriate Amazon listing or Brand Store destination. Review the packaging message, linked experience, and any data collection against applicable marketplace policies and privacy requirements. Configure Amazon Attribution where appropriate and verify whether the intended journey qualifies.
  5. Measure and improve. Track distributed magnets, engagement, and attributed Amazon purchases where available. Review eligible Brand Referral Bonus credits and total costs where measurable. Use an appropriate baseline or holdout where feasible to assess incremental retention rather than treating all attributed sales as incremental.
